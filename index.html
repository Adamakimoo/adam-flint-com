01  Approach

Three things I hold to, whatever the brief

Every one of these came out of a rollout that had to work the first time, usually with a deadline set by somebody else.

Design for the shift, not the seat

Frontline people learn between tasks, on the floor, on a phone. If a module assumes an empty hour and a desk, it will not get finished. Build for the twenty minutes that actually exist.

Evidence · EG Group, 14 modules delivered to staff nationally while stores stayed open

Compliance can still be worth finishing

Legislative and regulatory content is where learning design earns its keep. The obligation is fixed; the experience is not. Scenario, sequence and plain language do the work.

Evidence · Allianz, GICOP-aligned framework built with compliance, HR and digital transformation

Use the tools, don’t work around them

AI has moved storyboarding, scripting and review from weeks to days. I was in the first Copilot cohort at Allianz because the fastest way to understand a tool is to ship with it.

Evidence · Allianz, Microsoft Copilot pilot group · 25% cut in delivery time
